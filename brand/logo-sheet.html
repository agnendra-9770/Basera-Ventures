The idea

A B that is also a doorway

Basera means a dwelling, a place to rest for the night. The symbol draws the B of Basera as a Jaipur façade: the stem is a pillar, the upper counter is a jharokha window, and the lower counter is an arched doorway with its light on.

Both openings are cut in the same arch the website uses: vertical jambs, a small stepped shoulder and a pointed head, the profile of the city's palace and haveli doorways. It says home without drawing a house. The open door says welcome, and the B still reads at favicon size.

The wordmark sets BASERA in Fraunces, a high-contrast serif with crisp wedge serifs, and VENTURES in Instrument Sans, widely spaced. They are the same two typefaces the website uses.

Space and size

Give it room

  • Clear space on every side: at least the width of the B's pillar (X).
  • Smallest sizes: horizontal 140 px / 35 mm wide · stacked 90 px / 22 mm wide · symbol 16 px / 6 mm tall.
  • Use the files as supplied. Don't redraw, stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, recolour the door on a light ground, or set the name in another typeface.
  • On photographs, use the ivory version over a calm, darker area.
